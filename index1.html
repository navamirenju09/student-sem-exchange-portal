Eligibility and Requirements

General Criteria

  • Academic performance: Minimum CGPA of 7.0 (some programs ask for more).
  • Semester: You must have completed at least 2 semesters before going abroad.
  • Language: IELTS 6.0+ / TOEFL 80+ for English programs; basic language skills for other languages.
  • Backlogs: No pending backlogs (failed subjects).
  • Conduct: Good disciplinary record at your home university.
  • Passport: Valid for at least 6 months after the exchange ends.
  • Funds: Proof that you can pay for travel, stay and living costs.
